Pulse 1.0: the whole Pulse in its own window, signed in with this computer, with menus, search (Ctrl+K / ⌘K), back and forward, and an unread badge. A calmer quick panel: Today, Inbox and Chats as tabs with unread counts, a real messenger with bubbles and quick replies, and an inbox grouped into Needs you, Mentions and Updates. Settings is one simple sheet with plain switches. Windows pings now arrive reliably, and a single Fix button appears only if they cannot. New: Remove a late mark when Pulse saw you at the office first, Your day written for you, and your time wallet. Mac: from 1.0, updates keep you signed in without Keychain prompts; you may be asked to sign in once after this update.
