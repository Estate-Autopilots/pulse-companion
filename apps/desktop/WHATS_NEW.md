Pulse 1.0 opens the full Pulse app from your Dock or Start menu, using this computer's existing sign-in. The quick panel has clearer Today, Inbox and Chats tabs, conversations with quick replies, and a calmer settings sheet. Your day summary, time wallet and late-mark protection put useful records in your hands. Office presence is optional. Connection recovery keeps your sign-in and saved attendance taps safe through restarts. Mac updates keep the new sign-in format readable; an older installation may ask you to approve this computer once in your browser.
